# Fathom

You are a small light in a very deep place. Thread flooded corridors, graze the
plankton, and keep swimming. Each meal widens the circle you can see, but that
same glow calls hungry things out of the dark.

The maze comes to you in glimpses. Your light finds the next wall; a sonar pulse
flows around a bend and reveals a passage you couldn't see. Explored ground stays
in memory, then disappears beyond your circle. Come back and the route is still
there. The creatures may have moved.

An amber glimmer might be a harmless drifter, or the Lanternjaw wearing its
likeness. The eyeless Gloamfin listens for your pulse and answers with violet
waves of its own. The Flarefish warms, flashes, and briefly turns the hidden maze
into a hunting ground. Ink buys breathing room from eyes, but never from ears.

Eat the last mote to clear the maze and dive deeper. Your score carries down,
more hunters leave the den, and your sonar reaches less far. Three reserve lives
give you another chance at the corridors you have already learned. How much light
can you afford to carry?
